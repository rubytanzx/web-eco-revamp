The Story in 30 Seconds

01

The Challenge

Skills meant
leaving home.

Young people outside Ulaanbaatar had limited access to specialised technical education, leaving most without options close to home.

02

What ADB Supported

Bring training
closer.

ADB helped expand vocational facilities, equip workshops and establish accredited local programmes in regional centres.

03

What Changed

More opportunity,
locally.

Young people can now develop employment-ready skills without leaving their communities — most find work within six months.
